Looking for a Particular Book?

Can't find the book you're looking for? Tell us what you have in mind, and we'll do our best to track it down.

Our shop specialises in pre-loved books, unusual finds, and first editions. You can order a regular book from our catalogue or request a first-edition search.

How It Works

Choose the type of order, select a book category, and tell us the title you're looking for. If you have any special requests, add them in the information box.

For first-edition hunting, we charge 15.00 per search. We'll look through our sources and let you know if we find a suitable edition.

Visiting the Shop

If you would like to collect your order in person, choose a convenient date and time in the form. We're open

Monday–Saturday, 10:00–19:00.

And if you need help choosing a book, just ask. Henrik has read the first chapter of everything, and Ada can smell a fake first edition.
