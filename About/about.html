About Us Dog-Eared Pages Pre-loved books, freshly recommended. Dog-Eared Pages is a cozy little bookshop for people who love books not only for their stories, but for the stories behind them. You'll find used paperbacks, rare first editions, and unexpected literary finds on our shelves. And if the book you're looking for isn't here, we'll do our best to track it down for you. Our Team Henrik — Owner They say he's read the first chapter of every book in the shop. And, honestly, we believe it. Ada — Buyer She knows how to find interesting editions and, according to local legend, can even smell a fake first edition. Explore Our Shop At Dog-Eared Pages, finding a book can be an adventure in itself. Use our Map of the Shop to explore an interactive map of our interior, discover the layout of our shelves, and find the shop's special corners. Look out for bestsellers, rare finds, first editions, and unusual books marked on the map. Your next favorite book might be waiting exactly where you least expect it. Explore the Shop Map More Than Just Books We host a monthly book club with tea, hold a free children's story hour every Saturday, and offer gift wrapping with brown paper and string. And for those who love a surprise, there's our Blind Date with a Book — a mystery book wrapped so you won't know what you're getting until you open it. Come for a book. Stay for the next story.
